# 2. CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

The project combines three major ideas.

## A. Vaada-inspired customer protection

An insurance agent initiates a recording of the sales conversation, with consent, through the application.

After the conversation, the agent registers the customer, selects the insurance scheme and submits the registration.

The system transcribes the conversation and extracts promises made by the agent. It compares these promises against the actual terms and conditions of the selected insurance scheme.

The customer can subsequently log in, listen to the recording, inspect the transcript, review the promises and compare them with the original policy clauses before confirming the purchase.

This helps customers understand what was represented during the sale and whether those representations match the written policy.

## B. InsureChain policy-aware claims workflow

Customers submit claims against simulated active policies.

The system evaluates claims using explicit, deterministic policy rules. It detects late filings, excluded categories, above-limit claims, incomplete evidence and possible duplicate incidents.

Claims move through a strictly controlled state machine.

Every accepted decision and refused action is recorded in a tamper-evident audit history, with cryptographic hash anchoring on an EVM blockchain.

AI may assist with document extraction, narrative analysis and inconsistency detection, but AI-generated flags must never independently determine claim eligibility, authorize a state transition or prove fraud.

## C. ElevenLabs voice AI

Use ElevenLabs meaningfully for:
- Sales conversation transcription.
- Customer voice input.
- Spoken policy explanations.
- Spoken claim-status and decision explanations.
- Voice-assisted policy questions.
- Optional constrained conversational voice agents.

Voice AI must improve accessibility and explainability, not merely serve as a decorative integration.
